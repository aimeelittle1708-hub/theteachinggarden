About The Teaching Garden

The Teaching Garden is a simple resource-sharing space built for teachers and trainees. Upload classroom-ready materials, browse by subject and year group, and learn from each other through posts and comments.

Our values

  • Keep resources practical and classroom-ready
  • Be kind, constructive, and supportive
  • Share freely and credit others where possible

How moderation works

New uploads and comments may be held for approval to keep the hub safe and high-quality. You can still view your own pending comments while you wait.
